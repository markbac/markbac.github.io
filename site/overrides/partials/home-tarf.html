About the name

Tarf is Beta Cancri, the brightest star in the constellation Cancer. I have a strong interest in astronomy and physics, and wanted the site's name to carry it. Astronomy is grounded in science and discovery, and the star stands for the mix of exploration and knowledge this site aims to share. More resources and tools will be added as the site grows.
