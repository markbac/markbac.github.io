# Architecture Flashcards

<!-- more -->

## **1\. Problem Definition**

- What is the business problem or opportunity?
- Is the scope clearly defined and agreed?

---

## **2\. Stakeholder Identification**

- Who are the key stakeholders?
- Have you engaged with both business and technical stakeholders?

---

## **3\. Context Diagram**

- Have you defined the system boundaries?
- What external systems, actors, or processes interact with it?

---

## **4\. Requirements**

- Are the functional and non-functional requirements clear?
- Have they been prioritised and validated?

---

## **5\. Architectural Vision**

- What is the guiding vision for this architecture?
- Does it align with organisational strategy?

---

## **6\. Architectural Patterns**

- Which patterns are applicable (e.g., microservices, event-driven, layered)?
- Why are you choosing or rejecting specific patterns?

---

## **7\. Decision Records**

- Have you documented key architectural decisions?
- Are the trade-offs and rationale clear?

---

## **8\. System Quality Attributes**

- How will the architecture address performance, scalability, security, and maintainability?
- What are the primary quality attribute trade-offs?

---

## **9\. Modelling**

- Have you visualised the architecture using appropriate models (C4, UML, etc.)?
- Are the diagrams simple, clear, and focused?

---

## **10\. Data Flow**

- How does data flow through the system?
- Have you considered data integrity, consistency, and storage?

---

## **11\. Integration**

- How will the system integrate with other systems?
- Are integration points well-defined and robust?

---

## **12\. Security**

- Have you identified security threats and mitigations?
- How are authentication, authorisation, and data protection handled?

---

## **13\. Resilience and Availability**

- What happens if components fail?
- Is high availability or disaster recovery required?

---

## **14\. Performance**

- What are the performance expectations?
- Are there any known bottlenecks or constraints?

---

## **15\. Scalability**

- How will the system scale horizontally and/or vertically?
- Are there future growth considerations?

---

## **16\. Deployment Strategy**

- What is the deployment model (cloud, on-premises, hybrid)?
- How will you handle blue-green or canary deployments?

---

## **17\. Observability**

- Have you considered logging, monitoring, and alerting?
- How will you measure system health?

---

## **18\. Technical Debt**

- Are there areas of the architecture that introduce debt?
- How will you manage and pay it down?

---

## **19\. Standards and Guidelines**

- Are there coding, documentation, or architectural standards to follow?
- How will you ensure consistency across teams?

---

## **20\. Governance**

- What architectural governance processes are required?
- Are there any compliance or regulatory concerns?

---

## **21\. Evolution and Extensibility**

- Can the architecture evolve over time?
- How easy is it to add new features?

---

## **22\. API Design**

- Are APIs well-defined and RESTful, GraphQL, or event-driven as needed?
- Have you ensured proper versioning and backward compatibility?

---

## **23\. Vendor Independence**

- Are there any risks of vendor lock-in?
- What strategies will you use to maintain flexibility?

---

## **24\. Technology Selection**

- Is the technology stack suitable for the requirements?
- Have you considered longevity and community support?

---

## **25\. Cost**

- What are the cost implications of the architecture?
- Have you considered cloud costs, licensing, and operational expenses?

---

## **26\. Documentation**

- Is documentation lightweight but sufficient?
- Is it aimed at the right audience?

---

## **27\. Collaboration**

- Are you collaborating effectively with development, operations, and security teams?
- Have you communicated the architecture clearly?

---

## **28\. MVP and Iteration**

- What is the minimal viable product (MVP) for this architecture?
- How can it evolve iteratively?

---

## **29\. Risks and Assumptions**

- What are the key risks and assumptions?
- Are they documented and tracked?

---

## **30\. Feedback and Validation**

- How will you gather feedback and validate the architecture?
- Are there checkpoints for review and adaptation?
