# The First 90 Days: How to Nail Your Transition into a New Role

Michael D. Watkins’ _The First 90 Days_ is a playbook for successfully stepping into a new leadership role. The premise is simple: transitions are high-stakes, and the actions you take in the first three months set the foundation for long-term success.

<!-- more -->

Watkins outlines **10 key principles** to help new leaders navigate this critical period. These aren’t theoretical ideas—they’re practical steps to avoid common pitfalls, build credibility, and gain momentum.

---

## The 10 Principles of a Successful Transition

### 1\. Prepare Before You Start

- The transition begins _before_ day one.
- Research the company, team, culture, and challenges ahead of time.
- If possible, connect with key stakeholders early.

💡 **Practical takeaway:** Don’t show up on day one without a plan. Do your homework in advance.

### 2\. Accelerate Your Learning

- Your first priority is understanding the landscape, not making changes.
- Focus on learning the key players, culture, processes, and challenges.
- Ask structured questions and observe before forming strong opinions.

💡 **Practical takeaway:** Listen more than you talk. The better your understanding, the smarter your decisions will be.

### 3\. Match Your Strategy to the Situation

- Watkins outlines **four types of business situations**:
    - **Startup** – Building something new from the ground up.
    - **Turnaround** – Fixing something broken.
    - **Realignment** – Correcting a drifting but still functional organisation.
    - **Sustaining Success** – Keeping a strong system running well.
- Each requires a different leadership style—what works in a startup won’t work in a turnaround.

💡 **Practical takeaway:** Diagnose before acting. Use the right approach for the situation.

### 4\. Secure Early Wins

- Quick, visible successes help build credibility.
- Pick problems that are important but solvable within 90 days.
- Avoid overpromising or making drastic changes too soon.

💡 **Practical takeaway:** Identify 2-3 key areas where you can make an immediate impact.

### 5\. Negotiate Success with Your Boss

- Don’t assume you know what your boss expects—clarify it early.
- Define success in measurable terms and agree on priorities.
- Establish a communication rhythm that works for both of you.

💡 **Practical takeaway:** Proactively align expectations with your boss before misunderstandings arise.

### 6\. Achieve Alignment with Your Team

- Understand the existing team dynamics before making changes.
- Identify high performers, potential blockers, and team weaknesses.
- Be transparent about your expectations and leadership style.

💡 **Practical takeaway:** A misaligned team will slow you down. Make sure everyone is on the same page early.

### 7\. Build Key Alliances Across the Organisation

- Success isn’t just about your direct team—horizontal relationships matter.
- Identify key decision-makers, influencers, and stakeholders.
- Gain trust by listening, showing competence, and delivering results.

💡 **Practical takeaway:** Map out the organisation’s power structure and build the right relationships.

### 8\. Keep Your Balance

- Transitioning into a new role is high-pressure—don’t burn out.
- Prioritise the most critical tasks and delegate where possible.
- Keep perspective—mistakes will happen, and adaptation is key.

💡 **Practical takeaway:** You don’t need to fix everything immediately. Pace yourself.

### 9\. Accelerate Everyone’s Transition

- Your success is tied to how well your team adapts to your leadership.
- Support other new leaders in their transitions.
- Identify and remove barriers that slow people down.

💡 **Practical takeaway:** If you help others transition smoothly, your own job becomes easier.

### 10\. Make the Right First Impressions

- People form opinions quickly—be intentional about how you present yourself.
- Strike the right balance between confidence and humility.
- Actions speak louder than words—demonstrate competence early.

💡 **Practical takeaway:** First impressions stick, so approach your early days with awareness and strategy.

---

## Final Thoughts

The first 90 days in a new role aren’t just about survival—they’re about setting a foundation for long-term success. Watkins’ principles provide a structured approach to navigating this transition effectively.

The key takeaways? Learn fast, align with your boss and team, build relationships, secure quick wins, and don’t try to fix everything at once. Approach your first three months with a plan, and you’ll dramatically increase your chances of success.
