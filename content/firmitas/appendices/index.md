# Appendices

The appendices come from the first edition of Firmitas. Appendices A to G are its reference material. Appendices H and I are its sections on engineering practice across disciplines, and on quality, regulation and assurance. The second edition does not yet cover these subjects in chapters of their own.

The [Templates](../../templates/index.md) and [Glossary](../../glossary/index.md) sections of this site hold the current templates and terms.
