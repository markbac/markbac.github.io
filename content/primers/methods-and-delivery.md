# Methods and delivery primers

How a team works decides what it can deliver as much as the technology it uses. These primers introduce the common ways of planning, building, checking and learning.

## Planning and delivery

- [Software development life cycles](sdlc-models.md): waterfall, the V-model, spiral and iterative approaches.
- [Agile development](agile-development.md): the values and practices behind short, feedback-driven cycles.
- [Scrum and Kanban](scrum-vs-kanban.md): the two most common ways to run an agile team.

## Design and quality

- [Event storming](event-storming.md): a workshop for exploring a business domain.
- [Lightweight architecture evaluations](lightweight-architecture-evaluations.md): checking a design against what matters.
- [Testing strategies](testing-strategies.md): what to test, at which level.
- [Code review](code-review.md): getting value from a second pair of eyes.
- [Technical debt](technical-debt.md): naming it, measuring it and paying it down.
- [Docs as code](docs-as-code.md): treating documentation like software.

## Learning from problems

- [Root cause analysis](root-cause-analysis.md): finding out why something went wrong, not just what.

The [Guides](../guides/index.md) give step-by-step practice, and the [Templates](../templates/index.md) section has checklists for definitions of ready and done, reviews and postmortems.
