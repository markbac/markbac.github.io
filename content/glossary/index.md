# Glossary

Terms used across telecoms, networking, embedded and IoT engineering, software architecture and delivery, from A-law to ZigBee.

Each entry starts with a short definition. Acronyms give their expansion first, in bold. A sentence or two of detail follows where it helps, then links to related terms. The entries are deliberately short: they say what a term means and how it fits with the others, and leave the full story to the standards and books.

There is one page for each letter, and one for terms that start with a number or a symbol:
